ABOUT UNISYNC

Connecting Students,
Creating Change.

UniSync is an anonymous student feedback and complaint management system designed to help students share their concerns safely and help the university build a better environment.

WHO WE ARE

What is UniSync?

UniSync provides students with a simple and secure way to communicate their feedback, suggestions and complaints. The platform focuses on making student voices heard while maintaining privacy and ease of use.

By bringing feedback and communication into one platform, UniSync helps create a stronger connection between students and the university administration.

Our Mission

To provide students with a safe, accessible and anonymous platform where they can freely share their experiences, concerns and ideas.

Our Vision

To build a more responsive university environment where student feedback contributes to meaningful improvements and positive change.

WHY UNISYNC?
