How Can We Help You?

Explore the questions below to understand how UniSync works and how you can make the most of it.

01

Getting Started

Understanding the platform and creating an identity.

What is UniSync?

UniSync is a student feedback and complaint platform designed for RUET students. It provides a convenient way to communicate concerns, share suggestions and express appreciation to the Directorate of Students' Welfare.

Who can use UniSync?

UniSync is intended for students of Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). Students must use a valid RUET student email address to complete the verification process.

How do I get started?

Visit the platform and choose the service you want to use, such as submitting a complaint, suggestion or appreciation. You will be guided through the student verification process before accessing the submission form.

Why do I need to verify my student identity?

Verification ensures that the platform is being used by eligible RUET students. It helps maintain the reliability of submissions while keeping the identity separate from publicly displayed feedback.

02

Complaints & Tracking

Submitting concerns and following their progress.

How can I submit a complaint?

Select the complaint option from the homepage. Complete the student verification process if required, then describe your concern clearly in the complaint form and submit it.

Will my complaint be anonymous?

Yes. Complaints are presented through an anonymous student identity rather than displaying your name or student email. Verification is used to establish student eligibility, while your personal identity is kept separate from the complaint display.

Can I submit more than one complaint?

Yes. You can submit separate complaints for different concerns. Make sure each submission describes a distinct issue clearly to help the authorities review it.

How can I track my complaint?

Visit the complaint tracking section and provide your complaint information as requested. You can check the current status of your submitted complaint there.

What do the complaint statuses mean?

Pending: The complaint has been received and is awaiting review.

Under Investigation: The complaint is being examined by the responsible authority.

Resolved: The complaint has been addressed and marked as resolved.

Can I react to a complaint?

Yes. UniSync allows students to express agreement or disagreement with available complaints through the reaction feature. This helps highlight concerns shared by multiple students.

03

Privacy & Security

Understanding anonymity and responsible usage.

Will other students see my identity?

No. Your name and student email are not displayed alongside your complaint. Submissions are presented anonymously to other students.

Is my student email displayed with my complaint?

No. Your student email is used for verification and is not displayed publicly with your submitted feedback.

Can I submit false or inappropriate complaints?

UniSync encourages responsible and constructive feedback. Students should provide accurate information and avoid offensive language, harassment or deliberately misleading submissions.

04

Other Services

Suggestions, appreciation and additional assistance.

Can I submit suggestions through UniSync?

Yes. The suggestion feature allows you to share ideas and recommendations that could improve student welfare and university life.

Can I express appreciation to the university?

Yes. You can use the appreciation feature to acknowledge positive experiences, initiatives or contributions that have made a difference in student life.

How can I contact the Directorate of Students' Welfare?

You can find the Director's contact information, office location, phone number and official email address on our Contact page.
