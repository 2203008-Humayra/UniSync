Professional Profile

Academic background and professional achievements

Dr. Md. Rabiul Islam Sarker has been engaged in teaching at the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering since 2006. He completed his undergraduate and postgraduate studies at RUET and earned his PhD from the University of Adelaide, Australia, in 2015.

Throughout his academic career, he has served as Lecturer, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, and Professor. His teaching experience includes undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Renewable Energy Engineering, Heat Transfer, and other areas of Mechanical Engineering.

He contributed to a Higher Education Quality Enhancement Project (HEQEP), funded by the World Bank and the Government of Bangladesh through the University Grants Commission.
